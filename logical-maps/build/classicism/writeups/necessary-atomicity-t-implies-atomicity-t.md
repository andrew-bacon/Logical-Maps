## Premises

- **□Atomicity (type t).** Necessarily, every non-bottom proposition has an atom below it.

## Conclusion

- **Atomicity (type t).** Every non-bottom proposition has an atom below it.

## Proof

At each admitted closed instance $\phi$, T gives $\Box\phi\to\phi$. Apply this to every instance in the boxed premise, preserving its type and formula side conditions.
