# Full modalized domains: two-world chain

Source: Andrew Bacon, *The Broadest Necessity* (2018), §§7.3–7.4,
Definitions 7.7–7.11, Proposition 7.4, Corollaries 7.5–7.6; draft of
25 July 2017, pp. 37–41. Concrete instance and exposition by OpenAI Codex,
prompted by Andrew Bacon, 28 October 2026. No Lean verification.

Take W = {w,v}, with reflexivity and wRv but not vRw, designated world w.
Use a singleton individual domain with literal identity, the full
constant-domain function spaces, and all subsets of W at t. Identity of
propositions at u is agreement throughout R(u); higher-type identity is
agreement on related arguments throughout R(u). Every relational constant
of the map's signature denotes top.

The proposition p = {w} is true at w but impossible at v, so B fails at w.
Corollary 7.5 gives Functionality at every world, hence boxed Functionality.
The map derives BF and Tractarianism, including their necessitations.
This is therefore a B counterexample even with these stronger principles.

The general countermodel-transfer theorem, which covers every S4
non-theorem with an appropriate frame and valuation, is in
[classicism-s4-upper-bound.md](classicism-s4-upper-bound.md).

Fullness here is fullness of modalized function spaces. It is not an
assertion that this record meets the map's separate full-action-model
condition. No equivalence with an existing action-model record is needed.
