# No Pure Contingency ∧ Modalized Relational Choice ⇒ □Modalized Relational Choice

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.no_pure_contingency_and_modalized_relational_choice_imply_necessary_modalized_relational_choice`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026, from a suggestion of Cian Dorr; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 11 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **No Pure Contingency.** Each closed sentence of the pure language, if true, is necessary. This is a sentence schema, not a quantifier over propositions.
- **Modalized Relational Choice.** Every necessarily serial relation is entailed by a necessarily functional relation.

## Conclusion

- **□Modalized Relational Choice.** Necessarily, every necessarily serial relation is entailed by a necessarily functional relation.

## Proof

Every closed instance of Modalized Relational Choice is a closed sentence of the pure language. Given the principle, each is true, and No Pure Contingency makes a true closed pure sentence necessary.

## Notes

One of the uniform family by which No Pure Contingency carries a pure principle to its boxed form.
