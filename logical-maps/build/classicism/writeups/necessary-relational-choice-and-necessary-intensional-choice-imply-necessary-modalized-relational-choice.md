# □Relational Choice ∧ □Intensional Choice ⇒ □Modalized Relational Choice

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_relational_choice_and_necessary_intensional_choice_imply_necessary_modalized_relational_choice`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 11 October 2026, from a suggestion of Cian Dorr; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 11 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Relational Choice.** Necessarily, every serial binary relation has a functional subrelation.
- **□Intensional Choice.** Necessarily, if a property is necessarily instantiated, then some property entailing it is necessarily uniquely instantiated.

## Conclusion

- **□Modalized Relational Choice.** Necessarily, every necessarily serial relation is entailed by a necessarily functional relation.

## Proof

The argument of [[necessary-relational-choice-and-intensional-choice-imply-modalized-relational-choice]] is a C-proof of each instance from instances of □Relational Choice and Intensional Choice. Necessitate it; by 4 each instance of □Relational Choice gives its own necessitation, and K with the boxed premises gives the boxed conclusion.
