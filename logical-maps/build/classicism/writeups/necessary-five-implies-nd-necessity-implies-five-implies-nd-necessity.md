# □5-necessity implies ND-necessity ⇒ 5-necessity implies ND-necessity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_five_implies_nd_necessity_implies_five_implies_nd_necessity`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 14 October 2026, at Cian Dorr's direction; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□5-necessity implies ND-necessity.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\Box_{\ne}$.

## Conclusion

- **5-necessity implies ND-necessity.** Whatever is 5-necessary is ND-necessary.

## Proof

The T axiom for $\Box$: the boxed closed sentence implies the sentence.
