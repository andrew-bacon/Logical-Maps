# □Inextensible Comprehension ⇒ □Weakly Inextensible Comprehension

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_inextensible_comprehension_implies_necessary_wic`; produced by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 2 October 2026, from a suggestion of Cian Dorr; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), 2 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with an inextensible one.

## Conclusion

- **□Weakly Inextensible Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a weakly inextensible one.

## Proof

The proof of [[inextensible-comprehension-r-implies-weakly-inextensible-comprehension-r]] uses its premises only at finitely many closed instances and nothing beyond C. So for each closed instance of the conclusion, C proves the conditional from the corresponding premise instances to it. Necessitate that theorem and apply K with the boxed premise instances. Generalization over the admitted types is metalinguistic; no type quantifier is placed inside the box.
