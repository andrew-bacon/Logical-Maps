# □5-necessity implies ND-necessity ∧ □Truth implies 5-necessity ⇒ □Truth implies ND-necessity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_five_implies_nd_and_necessary_truth_implies_five_imply_necessary_truth_implies_nd`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 14 October 2026, at Cian Dorr's direction; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□5-necessity implies ND-necessity.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\Box_{\ne}$.
- **□Truth implies 5-necessity.** Necessarily, every truth is 5-necessary: necessary under every S5 operator. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\lambda p\, .\,p$.

## Conclusion

- **□Truth implies ND-necessity.** Necessarily, every truth is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_{\ne}=\lambda p\, .\,p$.

## Proof

Necessitate the composition $p\to\Box_5p\to\Box_{\ne}p$ and distribute the box over the conjunction of the two premises.
