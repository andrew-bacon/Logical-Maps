# Maximalism (pure) ∧ Necessity of Analysis ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 23 October 2026; the consistency premise witnessed by the model coalesced-two-extensional-arithmetics-root; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Zachary Goodsell, 23 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **Necessity of Analysis.** Every first-order analytical sentence is either true in every real number structure in every possible world or false in every real number structure in every possible world. One sentence for each type $\sigma$ and each first-order analytical sentence.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Let $T$ be C with Extensionality, Well-Ordering at every type and $I^*_e$, the theory of the components of coalesced-two-extensional-arithmetics-root, and let $A$ be the first-order analytical sentence saying that some natural number of the structure codes a proof of a contradiction from $T$, $\exists n\in N\,.\,\operatorname{Proof}_T(n,\bot)$, written with the structure's $N$, $+$ and $\times$. Let $\sigma$ be the type $((et)t)t$ of properties of the numbers at $e$, where Dedekind cuts live, and let $P$ be the closed pure sentence $\Diamond\exists\mathfrak R\,.\,(\operatorname{Real}_\sigma(\mathfrak R)\land A(\mathfrak R))\land\Diamond\exists\mathfrak R\,.\,(\operatorname{Real}_\sigma(\mathfrak R)\land\neg A(\mathfrak R))$, the negation of the instance of the Necessity of Analysis at $\sigma$ and $A$. $P$ is consistent with C: it is true at the root of coalesced-two-extensional-arithmetics-root, whose components $M_1\models\operatorname{Con}(T)$ and $M_2\models\neg\operatorname{Con}(T)$ each carry a real number structure of type $\sigma$, the Dedekind cuts over their numbers, in which $A$ is false and true respectively (the model's argument against the Necessity of Analysis). So Maximalism (pure), in its possibility form, gives $\Diamond P$, and by 4, $\Diamond\Diamond$ implying $\Diamond$, both conjuncts of $P$ hold: the instance of the Necessity of Analysis fails. So Maximalism (pure) and the Necessity of Analysis together give $\bot$.

## Notes

Maximalism is used at the one sentence $P$, whose consistency is witnessed by a model rather than by a principle of the map, as in maximalism-incompatible-with-modalized-relational-choice. Through the recorded implications it makes Maximalism in both forms, Possibility+ and Strong Possibility incompatible with the Necessity of Second-Order Analysis and with Analytic Necessitism, which imply the Necessity of Analysis.
