# □Two-hat necessity implies ND-necessity ∧ □5-necessity implies two-hat necessity ⇒ □5-necessity implies ND-necessity

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc., Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_two_hat_implies_nd_and_five_implies_two_hat_imply_five_implies_nd`; produced by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 14 October 2026, at Cian Dorr's direction; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Two-hat necessity implies ND-necessity.** Necessarily, whatever is two-hat necessary is ND-necessary: the distinctness-preserving modality agrees with its hat. In C this is the identity $\hat{\hat\Box}=\Box_{\ne}$.
- **□5-necessity implies two-hat necessity.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is two-hat necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\hat{\hat\Box}$.

## Conclusion

- **□5-necessity implies ND-necessity.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is ND-necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\Box_{\ne}$.

## Proof

Necessitate the proof of the unboxed result: Compose the two implications: $\Box_5p\to\hat{\hat\Box}p\to\Box_{\ne}p$. The chain is a theorem, so its instances are necessary, and K distributes.
