# Maximalism (pure) ∧ Non-contingency of the Continuum Hypothesis ⇒ ⊥

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: Misc.; produced by GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI), prompted by Daniel Kodsi, 23 October 2026 (the result, through a coalesced sum of components disagreeing about CH); the proof recorded here, through full-boolean-valued-atom-and-atomless, by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 24 October 2026; recorded by Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 24 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Maximalism (pure).** Every closed pure identity not provable in C is false. The non-theorem condition is metalinguistic.
- **Non-contingency of the Continuum Hypothesis.** Either necessarily every real number structure satisfies the continuum hypothesis, or necessarily every real number structure falsifies it: that every subproperty of the continuum either injects into the natural numbers or is in bijection with the continuum. One sentence for each type $\sigma$.

## Conclusion

- **⊥.** These premises cannot all hold together.

## Proof

Let $P$ be the negation of the instance of Non-contingency of CH at type $t$, $\Diamond\neg\forall\mathfrak R\, .\,(\operatorname{Real}_t(\mathfrak R)\to\operatorname{CH}(\mathfrak R))\land \Diamond\neg\forall\mathfrak R\, .\,(\operatorname{Real}_t(\mathfrak R)\to\neg\operatorname{CH}(\mathfrak R))$, a closed pure sentence. $P$ is consistent with C: it is true at the evaluation point of full-boolean-valued-atom-and-atomless, where CH takes opposite values in the two coordinates (the model's argument ch-contingent). So C does not prove $P=\bot$, and Maximalism (pure) gives $P\ne\bot$, that is $\Diamond P$. By 4, $\Diamond\Diamond$ implies $\Diamond$, so both conjuncts of $P$ hold, and the instance of Non-contingency of CH fails.

## Notes

Maximalism is used at the one sentence $P$, whose consistency is witnessed by a model, as in [[maximalism-incompatible-with-necessity-of-analysis]]. The witnessing model is defined by cases on CH in the ambient set theory, so the argument needs only ZFC.
