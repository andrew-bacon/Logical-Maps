# □Actuality ⇒ □5-necessities are two-hat necessary

<p class='cert'>Result — Source: The Bounds of Possibility (2021), Lean `Classicism.Map.necessary_actuality_implies_necessary_five_implies_two_hat`; produced by The Bounds of Possibility, Appendix D, Propositions D19–D21 (in HS4R); the weakening of the hypothesis to necessary Actuality observed by Cian Dorr and Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 14 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), prompted by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **□Actuality.** Necessarily, there is a true proposition that entails every true proposition.

## Conclusion

- **□5-necessities are two-hat necessary.** Necessarily, whatever is 5-necessary is two-hat necessary. In C this is the identity $\Box_5=\hat{\hat\Box}$.

## Proof

Proposition D21 of The Bounds of Possibility, Appendix D, proves $\forall p(\Box_5p\leftrightarrow\hat{\hat\Box}p)$ in HS4R. Its right-to-left direction uses only HS4. Its left-to-right direction uses, through Corollary D20 and Proposition D19, only Actuality ($\exists w\,W@w$) at the world of evaluation, and needs it at every world accessible from the actual one in order to obtain $\Box$NKJF$_{\langle\rangle}\hat{\hat\Box}$; $\Box$Actuality supplies exactly that. Necessitating gives the boxed principle.

## Notes

The boxed form of [[actuality-implies-five-implies-two-hat]].

Appendix D notes that the proof needs only this consequence of the rigidity axioms (Proposition C7). Corollary D23 is the same fact stated as $\hat{\hat{\hat\Box}}\equiv\hat{\hat\Box}$.
