# Vicinity ⇒ 5-necessity implies two-hat necessity

<p class='cert'>Conjecture — Source: Misc.; produced by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026; recorded by Claude Fable 5.1 (Anthropic), 14 October 2026.</p>

## Premises

- **Vicinity.** There is a true proposition that entails the possibility of each true proposition.

## Conclusion

- **5-necessity implies two-hat necessity.** Whatever is 5-necessary is two-hat necessary.

## Notes

Conjectured by Cian Dorr, 14 October 2026. Actuality suffices (see the proved result), and so does Vicinity with BF at type $t$. Without BF, the two places where the Actuality proof uses the atom resist the Vicinity substitution: the characterization $\hat{\hat\Box}p\leftrightarrow\Box(\hat\Diamond w\to p)$ needs $\Box(\hat\Diamond w\to\hat\Diamond q)$ for an arbitrary true $q$, which is B for $\Box_{\ne}$ at $q$; and step 1 of Proposition D19 applies the atom to the truth $\forall z\,\Box(\hat\Diamond w\to Yz)$, which is ND-necessary only if $\Box_{\ne}$ already commutes with $\forall z$. A countermodel would need Vicinity without Actuality at a world where the two-hat cluster is non-principal; the paired-individuals and star-graph models fail Vicinity.
