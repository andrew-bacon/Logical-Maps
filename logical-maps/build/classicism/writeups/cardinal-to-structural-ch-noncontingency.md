# □Rigid Comprehension ∧ Intensional Choice ∧ Necessary Peano conditions (at-least numbers) ∧ Non-contingency of Cardinal CH ⇒ Non-contingency of the Continuum Hypothesis

<p class='cert'>Conjecture — Source: Misc.; produced by Andrew Bacon (proposed equivalence); OpenAI Codex, prompted by Andrew Bacon (explicit candidate assumptions); recorded by OpenAI Codex, prompted by Andrew Bacon.</p>

## Premises

- **□Rigid Comprehension.** Necessarily, every relation, including a proposition, is coextensive with a rigid one.
- **Intensional Choice.** If a property is necessarily instantiated, then some property entailing it is necessarily uniquely instantiated.
- **Necessary Peano conditions (at-least numbers).** Necessarily, at-least zero is not a successor of an at-least natural number, and successor is injective on those numbers.
- **Non-contingency of Cardinal CH.** Cardinal CH for the specified natural-number carrier is either necessarily true or necessarily false.

## Conclusion

- **Non-contingency of the Continuum Hypothesis.** Either necessarily every real number structure satisfies the continuum hypothesis, or necessarily every real number structure falsifies it: that every subproperty of the continuum either injects into the natural numbers or is in bijection with the continuum. One sentence for each type $\sigma$.

## Notes

One direction of the proposed equivalence of the two non-contingency principles under the displayed candidate assumptions. These assumptions have not yet been proved sufficient. For the forward direction one needs, at every carrier type, necessary comparison of every real structure's natural-number carrier and continuum with N and S. For the converse one also needs a necessarily available real structure at some specified sufficiently high type; otherwise the structural universal quantifiers can be vacuous. Current-world bijections alone do not transfer modal claims. Goodsell's arithmetic rigidity result addresses the number carrier, not the real-structure construction. See Background, Cardinal CH and bounded forcing.
